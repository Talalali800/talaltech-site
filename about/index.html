About us

TalalTech is a software studio. We build apps for people and organizations who want tools that work well and treat their information with care.

What we make

We focus on a few apps and try to do each one properly:

  • Calamus3 — Chat one-to-one and in groups with messages encrypted on your phone. No phone number, no email — just a username.
  • AdaptivePlanner — Tasks move around your meetings, prayer times and deadlines automatically — no more shuffling your day by hand.
  • Granteez — Granteez finds the grants your nonprofit actually qualifies for and drafts a first application grounded in your own documents.

How we think about privacy

Privacy is not a feature we add at the end. In Calamus3, messages are end-to-end encrypted and stored on your phone, so we could not read them even if asked. AdaptivePlanner reads your calendars on your phone and never uploads them. Granteez encrypts every organization’s documents separately.

Across all our apps, we do not sell personal information, we explain what we collect in plain language, and you can delete your data from inside the app.
