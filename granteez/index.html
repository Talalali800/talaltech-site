FAQsAnswers to common questions
Who is Granteez for?

Nonprofits and charities — especially small teams without a full-time grant writer.

Does Granteez make things up in the application?

No. Drafts are written only from the documents your organization uploads, and every claim is cited back to its source so your team can check it.

How does pricing work?

A straightforward fee per application, charged only when you choose to unlock a draft. There are no seat licences to negotiate.

Will you use our documents to train AI?

No. Your documents are encrypted for your organization alone, are never shared with other organizations and are never used for training.

Does Granteez submit applications on its own?

No. Nothing goes out until someone on your team approves it, and approving requires a second sign-in step.

How to use GranteezGet started in a few steps
  1. Create your organizationSign up, invite your teammates and set up two-step sign-in.
  2. Complete your profileDescribe your mission, programs and where you work, so matches fit.
  3. Upload your documentsAdd the reports, budgets and program descriptions a funder would ask about.
  4. Review your matchesEach opportunity is scored against your profile, so you can focus on the ones worth applying to.
  5. Unlock and edit the draftUnlock an application to see its full, cited draft, then review and edit it with your team.
  6. Approve and submitApprove with your second sign-in step when the application is ready to go.
