Delete your AdaptivePlanner account

AdaptivePlanner is made by TalalTech. You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from inside the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open AdaptivePlanner.
  2. Open the Settings tab and tap Profile.
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete account. (If you never created an account, it says Erase all my data.)
  4. Tap Delete permanently to confirm. Everything is deleted straight away — there is no waiting period.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes your account (email address and password) and everything stored with it: your tasks, repeating tasks, calendar events you added in the app, your working hours and other settings, your prayer and religious-observance preferences (including location coordinates for prayer times) and the prayers you moved to a later time. The copy of your plan kept on your phone for quick loading and for the home-screen widget, and the prayers you ticked off as prayed, are erased from your phone too.

Deleting some of your data without deleting your account

  • A single task — open it and tap Delete task.
  • Phone calendars — Settings → Calendars → open your linked calendars → Disconnect. Calendar events are only ever read on your phone and are never stored by us.
  • Prayer settings and location — in Settings, clear the Location coordinates, set Religion to None, and tap Save.

What is kept

Nothing is kept by us after deletion, except that our database provider’s routine backups can hold deleted data for a short time until they expire. If you contacted us by email, that conversation stays in our mailbox unless you ask us to delete it.

Can’t sign in?

Email contact@thequreshico.ca from the address on your account and we will delete it for you. Never created an account? Then your data isn’t linked to any email — tap Erase all my data in the app (Settings → Profile) before uninstalling it, and it’s all removed.

For everything else about your data, see the AdaptivePlanner privacy policy.
