Delete your Calamus3 account

Calamus3 is made by TalalTech. You can delete your account yourself, at any time, from inside the app.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Calamus3 and sign in.
  2. Open the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account permanently.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted straight away — there is no waiting period.

What is deleted

Deleting your account removes your account and profile, encryption keys, anything still waiting to be delivered, your chat backup, uploads, one-to-one conversations, contact requests, call records, reports and problem reports, notification subscriptions, and your coins, perks and cash-out records. Groups you created are deleted too, and the chats on the phone you delete from are erased.

Deleting some of your data without deleting your account

  • Profile picture, display name, bio and country — change or remove them in Profile.
  • Chat backup — Settings → Chat backup → Turn off and delete backup removes the copy on our server.
  • Chats — delete a chat, or individual messages, on your phone. They are stored only on your phone.
  • Signed-in devices — sign any of them out from the device list in Settings.
  • Discover — opt out of being suggested to others in Settings.

What is kept

Nothing is kept by us after deletion, except that our database provider’s routine backups can hold deleted data for a short time until they expire. Google AdMob and Tremendous keep their own records of ads shown and payouts sent, under their own policies. Unspent coins are lost — cash out first if you want to keep their value.

Can’t sign in?

Email contact@thequreshico.ca with your username and we will help. Calamus3 accounts have no email address or phone number attached, so we may ask you to confirm the account is yours before deleting it.

For everything else about your data, see the Calamus3 privacy policy.
